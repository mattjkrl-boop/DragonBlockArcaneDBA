# Phase 6 Before Audit (Nov 1, 2026): mob physics

From FEATURE-WAVE-SCOPE.md: there are no custom hostile mobs yet; DBA entities are ability projectiles, domains, effects, Shenron, Porunga, and Nimbus. Mob physics therefore means (a) a knockback, launch, juggle, and ragdoll-lite response system for vanilla mobs and players hit by DBA abilities, and (b) groundwork future DBA mobs inherit. Today, knockback is whatever each ability hardcodes; identical hits feel different per weapon, heavy mobs and bosses take the same launch as a chicken, nothing juggles, nothing bounces or splats, and deaths are the vanilla tip-over no matter what killed the mob.

Plan (core slice):
- movement/MobPhysics (common, server): weight classes from knockback resistance and max health (LIGHT, HEAVY, BOSS). A single Fabric AFTER_DAMAGE hook catches DBA-sourced hits (attacker is a player holding a DbaGeoWeapon, or the damage entity lives in the DBA entity package) at 6+ damage and applies a weight-scaled launch along the hit direction: light mobs pop and juggle, heavy mobs stagger, bosses barely move. Tracked mobs get juggle gravity clamping (fall speed capped while the juggle window lives), one ground bounce with impact damage, and one wall splat with impact damage and a brief slow. Players receive launches only; their landing feel belongs to the Phase 5 system.
- Ragdoll-lite death: the hook records the last DBA hit per entity; AFTER_DEATH broadcasts a DeathTumblePayload (entity id, strength) to tracking players when the killing context was a DBA hit within 60 ticks. Client side, a duck-typed render state (same pattern as DbaPlayerState) carries the strength into LivingEntityRenderer.setupRotations, which adds a forward tumble on top of the vanilla death fall, scaled by strength. Client visual only; server state stays vanilla.
- Debris generalization stays a later slice: the OxFissure, ZGravitySlam, and KingsSlam patterns remain per-ability for now, recorded here as the known remainder alongside boss stagger animations.
