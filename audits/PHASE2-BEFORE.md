# Phase 2 Before Audit (Nov 1, 2026): built-in shaders

What exists: one post-processing system, DbaPostFx (client fx), rendering vanilla PostChains after the world. Three fragment shaders in shaders/post/: dba_eclipse (violet grade plus crimson rim, driven by the Storm of Darkness effect, 4 stepped chain JSONs eclipse_1..4), dba_grayscale (cool desaturation, driven by Temporal Stasis, time_stop_1..4), dba_shockwave (expanding chromatic ring, 8 stepped frames shockwave_0..7, triggered from the shake payload at intensity >= 1.2). Uniforms are static per chain JSON, so intensity animates by stepping between pre-made chains. The whole system fails safe (one render exception disables it) and honors the effects density config.

What is missing for "true built-in shaders": nothing responds to the mod's core verb, charging. There is no shader feedback while the player charges a weapon or technique, and no danger feedback at low health. The weapon glowmask textures from Phase 1 are also not yet consumed by any emissive pass; that is an effects concern and moves to Phase 3, recorded here so it is not lost.

Plan: two new post shaders in the same proven pipeline. dba_ki_surge: energy rim, saturation and contrast lift, and a chromatic fringe at the screen edges while the local player is using an item (the universal charge verb, readable on the client with no new networking), stepped ki_surge_1..4 by charge duration. dba_danger: deep red closing vignette at low health, stepped danger_1..3 at 35, 20, and 10 percent health. Both wire into DbaPostFx tick and render alongside the existing three, behind the same fail-safe and config gate.
