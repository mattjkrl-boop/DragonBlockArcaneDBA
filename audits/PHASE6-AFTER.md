# Phase 6 After Audit (Nov 1, 2026): mob physics core

Built:
- movement/MobPhysics (common, server): weight classes from knockback resistance and max health (LIGHT under 90 hp, HEAVY 90 to 400 or kb resist 0.45+, BOSS 400+ or 0.9+). One ServerLivingEntityEvents AFTER_DAMAGE hook detects DBA hits (player holding a DbaGeoWeapon, or a damage entity from the DBA entity package) at 6+ damage and launches the target along the hit direction, power 0.30 + damage x 0.028 capped at 1.45, scaled by class (1.0 / 0.45 / 0.12) with class lift factors. Tracked non-player mobs (36 tick window) get juggle fall clamping at -0.22, one ground bounce at 42 percent rebound with impact damage capped at 6 plus dust and the small-fall sound, and one wall splat with damage capped at 8 and a brief strong slow. Players take launches only.
- Ragdoll-lite: the hook records the last DBA hit per entity; AFTER_DEATH broadcasts DeathTumblePayload (registered clientbound in DbaNetwork) to tracking players when the death followed a DBA hit within 60 ticks, strength 0.4 + damage/22 capped at 1.5. Client TumbleFx stores strengths by entity id for 30 ticks; a DbaTumbleState duck interface on LivingEntityRenderState (same pattern as DbaPlayerState, mixin registered in the client mixins json) is stamped in extractRenderState and consumed in setupRotations, adding a forward tumble up to 260 degrees x strength across the death animation. Client visual only.

Audit: javac against the real jars. With the full library set on the classpath (jspecify and Netty jars from the mega-mod audit-libs, which the hand-rolled classpath initially lacked), DeathTumblePayload, MobPhysics, and TumbleFx compile with zero errors. The Mixin files remain at parity with existing code (Mixin annotation packages are supplied by Loom in a real build and absent from the raw classpath; the pre-existing CameraShakeMixin produces the identical errors). Mixin target signatures were verified from the 26.2 jar before writing (setupRotations and extractRenderState shapes, LivingEntityRenderState.deathTime field). This is an audit result, not a Gradle build; the build remains waived per BUILD-VERIFY.md.

Known remainder at the time: debris generalization for slam abilities (OxFissure, ZGravitySlam, KingsSlam patterns) and boss stagger animations. Both were completed the same day at Carter's direction: see audits/MOVEMENT-MOB-FOLLOWUP-AFTER.md.
