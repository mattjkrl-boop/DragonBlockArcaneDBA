## Shared render base (applies to every section below)

- Every blade renderer builds custom mesh geometry in code through
  submitCustomGeometry over one shared helper,
  client/render/ki/KiRenderHelper.java. Its canvas is a flat white texture,
  textures/entity/ki_white.png (KiRenderHelper.java:20), so all color is
  per-vertex color at FULL_BRIGHT 0xF000F0 (KiRenderHelper.java:37).
- There is no true additive blending in the current code. kiAdditiveType()
  (KiRenderHelper.java:27-28) returns RenderTypes.entityTranslucentEmissive,
  the identical type kiRenderType() returns (KiRenderHelper.java:32-33). The
  helper comment (KiRenderHelper.java:23-26) says the eyes shader was dropped
  on purpose because it ignores vertex alpha. Any section below that says a
  glow pass uses kiAdditiveType means a second normal-blended copy, not an
  additive one.
- The storm standard the sections audit against: (1) layered rotating
  particle bands with a dense bright core, a mid layer, wispy edges, and
  distance scaling; (2) hundreds of individual debris pieces tumbling on
  helical orbit paths; (3) world interaction, dust at the base, ground
  scarring, mobs pulled in, lifted, and flung out; (4) atmosphere, darkened
  sky, dimmed light, haze, branching lightning; (5) a full lifecycle, build
  up, peak, slow dissipation with debris raining down, and no pops at either
  end.

### Numbers holding the spectacle back
- Crescent lifetime `10` ticks on a hit and `8` ticks on a freeform trail (KatanaItem.java:244, KatanaItem.java:265) against a renderer that needs the first 30 percent of life just to open (SwiftCrescentRenderer.java:69). One second for build, peak, and total dissipation leaves no slow decay phase, and the shock rings are cut off at progress `0.38f`, about 8 ticks in (HeavenSplitterRenderer.java:125, HeavenSplitterRenderer.java:126).
- Instant one tick damage in both finishers. Flashdraw teleports and queues all damage through a single server execute block with no per target delay between targets (KatanaItem.java:213, KatanaItem.java:226, KatanaItem.java:229), and Heaven Splitter applies its full damage to every target in one loop in the release tick (KatanaItem.java:384, KatanaItem.java:386). ### Numbers holding the spectacle back
- Lightning damage is one tick, all or nothing: `if (this.life == 12)` at AzureLightningEntity.java:56 deals the full `450.0f` (from AzureStormEntity.java:165) or `350.0f` (from AzureTornadoEntity.java:109) instantly. A strike that reads as a 44 block tall branching bolt (AzureLightningRenderer.java:85) is mechanically a single instant hit in a plus or minus 3.5 block box (AzureLightningEntity.java:58-59). There is no damage over time, no chain, no second pulse.
- Storm lifetime is flat at `220` ticks (AzureStormEntity.java:30) regardless of charge, while the charge that feeds it spans `MAX_TEMPEST_CHARGE_TICKS = 300` ticks (AzureDragonSwordItem.java:45). A 0.5 second tap (`heldTicks >= 10`, AzureDragonSwordItem.java:348) and a 15 second full charge produce the same 11 second storm; only radius (AzureDragonSwordItem.java:350) and the giant tornado flag (AzureDragonSwordItem.java:351) differ. Eleven seconds is also too short for the standard's build, peak, slow dissipate arc: the renderer spends 20 ticks fading in and 40 ticks fading out (AzureStormRenderer.java:72-73), leaving 160 ticks, 8 seconds, at full presence.
- Storm cooldown is shorter than the storm: `60 + (int) (chargeRatio * 60)` ticks (AzureDragonSwordItem.java:377) is 60 to 120 ticks, 3 to 6 seconds, against a 220 tick storm (AzureStormEntity.java:30). Nothing in releaseUsing or in AzureStormEntity prevents a second storm while the first is alive, so domains can overlap and stack their `% 25` strike cadence (AzureStormEntity.java:159) instead of one storm reading as a singular event.
- Mini tornado life of `60` ticks (AzureTornadoEntity.java:27) against a renderer fade of 10 ticks in and 20 ticks out (AzureTornadoRenderer.java:68-69) means a third of its life is fade out and it is at full visual strength for about 30 ticks, 1.5 seconds. Its damage cadence `% 10` at AzureTornadoEntity.java:93 gives at most 6 damage ticks of `25.0f` (AzureTornadoEntity.java:97).
- Sonic Quake is a 0.9 second effect: `maxLifetime = 18` at AzureSonicQuakeEntity.java:18 and renderer `maxLife = 18.0f` at AzureSonicQuakeRenderer.java:54. Its `450.0f + strength * 2.5` damage (AzureDragonSwordItem.java:243) lands in full on the trigger tick at AzureDragonSwordItem.java:250, with no telegraph, no delay, and no falloff inside the `8.0` radius (AzureDragonSwordItem.java:244-247): a target at 7.9 blocks takes the same damage as a target at the center.
