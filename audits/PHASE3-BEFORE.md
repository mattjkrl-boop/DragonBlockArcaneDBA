# Phase 3 Before Audit (Nov 1, 2026): effects

What exists: four custom particle types (spark, ember, void_smoke, wind), all rendered by one configurable class, DbaGlowParticle (Style records carry color ramp, life, size, growth, gravity, drag, additive flag), registered in DbaParticles (common) and DbaParticleProviders (client), with a 4-frame sprite set each. SwingTrailRenderer draws per-weapon trails in the trail colors. AuraRenderer renders form auras. 83 client ability renderers cover techniques.

Correction to the Phase 2 note: the weapon glowmasks are NOT unconsumed. Verified from the real GeckoLib 5.5.3 jar: AutoGlowingGeoLayer (already attached to every weapon renderer in DbaGeoWeaponRenderers) resolves its texture through RenderUtil.getEmissiveResource, which replaces ".png" with "_glowmask.png" in the model texture path. The Phase 1 naming (textures/item/<id>_geo.png with <id>_geo_glowmask.png) matches exactly, so every migrated weapon's glow strips already render fullbright. What a static layer cannot do is pulse; the pulse has to come from particles.

What is missing: charging has a screen shader (Phase 2) but no world-space effect. Nobody can see another player charging up. Plan: a fifth particle type, ki_mote (soft additive mote, slow rise, long life), plus a client ChargeFx ticker: for every player in the level who is using an item, motes spiral inward around them at a rate scaled by the effects density config; when a charge of 10+ ticks ends, a ring of sparks bursts out. Purely client side, reads only synced entity state, no new networking.
