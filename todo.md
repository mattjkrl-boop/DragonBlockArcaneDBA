# Dragon Block Arcane DBA — Remaining Work

## Character Models
Race OBJ meshes render on the player, and they still need finished per-race models, form models, and real textures. BlockBench sources cover Saiyan, Half-Saiyan, and Yardrat only. Every other race texture in the player-model pack is the same placeholder.

## Character Animations
One shared `player.animation.json` drives every race on the humanoid skeleton. Per-race clips and transformation animations still need to be made and loaded. The copied files under `better_player_model` are not the live set.

## V Menu Redesign
The V screen is a code-drawn panel with Stats, Forms, Skills, Craft, and Emotes. It still needs a redesigned layout and textured UI.

## Ki Weapons/Ki Attacks (Ki Barrier)
Custom Ki can fire blast, barrage, beam, spiral beam, disk, laser, and explosion. A Ki Barrier move is still missing.

## Workable customization of Ki
The Craft tab saves type, color, Ki percent, and barrage into three slots that fire. Charge time, size, and a barrier option still have to be added before a custom move covers the intended controls.

## Dragon Wish Screen/Ritual Formation
Placing seven Earth or Namek balls within five blocks summons Shenron or Porunga and opens a three-wish panel (wealth, power, immortality). A ritual formation and a finished wish screen are still missing.

## Player Skills
The Skills tab shows a 50-node tree. Forty-eight nodes are unnamed placeholders, and every race shares that same tree. Real per-race skills still need names, effects, and their own trees.

## DBZ Mob Models
Enemy mobs still need models and entities. Current living characters are Shenron, Porunga, Flying Nimbus, and the Otherworld guide. Only the two dragons have geo models.

## Items, Robots, Weapons, Armor
Nineteen signature weapons have models and attack visuals. Battle armor (chest, legs, boots) and a Piccolo outfit exist as vanilla humanoid armor. Robots, the rest of the item and armor sets, and custom armor models are still open.

## Crafting Recipes
Recipes are furnace and blasting for aetherium, bauxite, dragstone, silver, tin, and steel. Shaped recipes for weapons, armor, and the other gear are still missing.
