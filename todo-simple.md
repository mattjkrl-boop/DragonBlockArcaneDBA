# Remaining Work

- **Character Models** — Race meshes render, but finished per-race and form models plus real textures are still missing. BlockBench sources cover Saiyan, Half-Saiyan, and Yardrat only.
- **Character Animations** — Every race plays one shared `player.animation.json`. Per-race clips and transformation animations are still missing.
- **V Menu Redesign** — The V screen is still a code-drawn Stats / Forms / Skills / Craft / Emotes panel and needs a redesigned textured UI.
- **Ki Weapons/Ki Attacks (Ki Barrier)** — Blast, beam, disk, laser, spiral, and explosion fire. Ki Barrier is still missing.
- **Workable customization of Ki** — Craft saves type, color, Ki percent, and barrage into three firing slots. Charge time, size, and barrier are still missing.
- **Dragon Wish Screen/Ritual Formation** — Seven balls within five blocks summon Shenron or Porunga and open three hardcoded wishes. A ritual formation and a finished wish screen are still missing.
- **Player Skills** — The skill tree is 50 nodes, 48 of them unnamed placeholders, shared by every race. Real per-race skills are still missing.
- **DBZ Mob Models** — Enemy mobs are still missing. Living characters are Shenron, Porunga, Flying Nimbus, and the Otherworld guide.
- **Items, Robots, Weapons, Armor** — Nineteen signature weapons have models and visuals. Armor is battle armor (no helmet) and a Piccolo outfit. Robots and the rest of the gear are still missing.
- **Crafting Recipes** — Only ore and ingot smelting and blasting exist. Shaped recipes for weapons, armor, and other gear are still missing.
